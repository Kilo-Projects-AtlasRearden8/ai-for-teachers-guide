# 7. Privacy and Safety

This is the most important guide in the manual. Read it before you paste anything into an AI tool.

## The golden rule

**Never paste student names, grades, ID numbers, or any personal student information into an AI tool.**

Treat every AI chat like a public whiteboard: only write what you would feel comfortable sharing openly. AI providers may keep, review, or use what you type, even on free accounts.

## What NOT to paste

- Student first or last names
- Student ID numbers
- Grades or scores
- Comments about specific students (e.g., "Maria struggles with reading")
- IEPs, 504 plans, or health information
- Anything else that could identify a student

## What IS safe to paste

- Generic topics ("fractions," "the water cycle," "the American Revolution")
- Grade levels ("fourth grade")
- Lesson objectives and standards
- Your own observations written without names ("some students need more practice with decimals")

## If your school has rules

Check with your administrator or technology director before using AI for schoolwork. Some districts ban specific tools or require approved platforms. Follow your school's policy first.

## FERPA, in plain English

FERPA is a U.S. privacy law that protects student education records. Pasting student names or grades into an AI tool can risk disclosing protected information. This manual is not legal advice. When in doubt, keep student information out of AI tools entirely, and ask your school for guidance.

## Reduce data sharing

Many AI tools let you turn off training on your conversations in the settings. Look for options like "disable model training," "incognito mode," or "do not use my conversations to improve the model." Turn them on if available.

## AI is not an expert

- AI can get facts wrong. Check everything before giving it to students or parents.
- AI is not a doctor, lawyer, or counselor. Never use it for medical advice, legal decisions, or student evaluations.
- You are responsible for what you send home or hand out.

## Quick checklist before every chat

- [ ] No student names in the prompt
- [ ] No grades or scores in the prompt
- [ ] No identifying details about individual students
- [ ] I will fact-check the output before using it
- [ ] I am following my school's AI policy

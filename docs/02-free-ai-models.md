# 2. Free AI Models

You do not need to pay to use AI for teaching. This guide lists free options and how to choose one. Availability and pricing change, so always check the tool's website before signing up.

## What "free" usually means

Most AI chat tools offer a free tier with limits, for example:

- A set number of messages per day or month
- Access to an older or smaller model for free
- Slower responses during busy times

You typically do not need a credit card to start. If a site asks for payment before you can send a single message, it is probably not a free-tier tool — look elsewhere.

## Kinds of free tools

**Web chatbots.** Open a website, sign in with an email, and start typing. Many well-known AI assistants have a free tier. These are the easiest to start with.

**Local AI (runs on your computer).** Tools like Ollama or LM Studio let you download and run models on your own machine. Nothing you type leaves your computer. This is the most private option, but you need a reasonably modern computer and some free disk space.

**School-provided tools.** Your district may already license an AI tool for teachers. Check with your technology director before signing up for anything else.

## How to choose

Start with one web-based free tool. Use it for a week. If you worry about privacy or want to use it a lot, try a local model next. Do not create accounts on five tools at once — pick one and learn it.

## Safety check before you sign up

- Is there a free tier? (Yes/No)
- Does it require a credit card just to try? (No = good)
- Can you turn off training on your conversations? (See `07-privacy-safety.md`)
- Does your school allow it?

If any answer makes you uncomfortable, pick a different tool.

## Your first session

1. Open the tool's website in your browser.
2. Sign up with an email address only — do not add your school, phone number, or payment details unless required.
3. Start a new chat.
4. Send a simple prompt: "I am a teacher. Suggest three quick warm-up activities for a middle-school science class."
5. Read the response, edit it, and try again with different wording if needed.

## Remember

Free models can still make mistakes. Always fact-check anything you plan to use with students or send to parents. And never paste student names, grades, or other personal details into any AI tool.

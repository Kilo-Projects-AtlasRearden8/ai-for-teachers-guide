Welcome

You do not need to be technical. You do not need to pay. This manual walks you through using free AI models for everyday teaching work — lesson plans, worksheets, quiz questions, and emails to parents — in plain English, one step at a time.

What is inside

  • Getting started — what AI is, picking a free model, your first prompt
  • Free AI models — free tools you can use today, no credit card required
  • Lesson plans — step-by-step with an example prompt you can copy
  • Worksheets — draft questions and answer keys in minutes
  • Quizzes — multiple choice and short answer, with a separate answer key
  • Parent emails — warm, short emails in plain English
  • Privacy and safety — the most important rules, read first
  • Printable templates — fill-in-the-blank forms you can print

The golden rule: never paste student names, grades, ID numbers, or any personal student information into an AI tool. Treat an AI chat like a public whiteboard.

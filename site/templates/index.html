Markdown versions

The same templates are in docs/templates/ as Markdown files: lesson-plan.md, worksheet.md, quiz.md, parent-email.md. Open them in any text editor and print.

How to print

  1. Open a template page in your browser.
  2. Press Ctrl+P (Windows) or Command+P (Mac).
  3. Navigation and headers hide automatically, leaving a clean form.

Never write student names or grades on templates you might share or scan into an AI tool.
